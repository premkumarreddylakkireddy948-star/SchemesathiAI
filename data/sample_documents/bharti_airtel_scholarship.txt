================================================================================
BHARTI AIRTEL FOUNDATION
SCHEME GUIDELINES: BHARTI AIRTEL SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM (MERIT-CUM-MEANS)
================================================================================
Scheme Code: CORP-EDU-AIRTEL-2024
Category: Higher Education & Corporate Scholarships
Target Group: Meritorious students from economically weaker families, with a focus on girl students pursuing B.Tech / B.E. degrees in Technology, AI, Data Science, Telecom, Electronics, and Computer Engineering in top NIRF-ranked institutions (such as IITs, NITs, and premier state tech universities).

1. SCHEME OBJECTIVE:
To provide 100% financial support to promising students from low-income families so they can pursue higher education in technology and engineering without financial burden.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Candidate must be a citizen of India.
- Enrolled as a first-year student in a full-time B.Tech / B.E. program in Computer Science, AI, Data Science, Electronics, Telecommunication, or allied engineering disciplines in top institutes (IITs, NITs, and top state engineering colleges).
- Annual Family Income: Total annual family income from all sources must NOT exceed ₹6,00,000 (Rupees Six Lakh per annum).
- Academic Performance: High JEE Advanced or JEE Main rank and minimum 70% marks in 12th Board Examinations.
- Preference given to female students and SC/ST/PwD applicants.

3. BENEFITS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE:
- 100% Tuition Fee Waiver: Full coverage of college tuition fees paid directly to the institute.
- Hostel & Mess Allowance: Full coverage of campus hostel accommodation and dining charges.
- One-time Laptop Grant: Laptop or learning tablet provided for academic work.
- Mentorship & Industry Exposure: Executive mentorship program by Senior Leaders and Engineers at Bharti Airtel.

4. MANDATORY REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. College Admission Allotment Letter & Fee Structure.
2. Entrance Exam Scorecard & Rank Card (JEE Main / JEE Advanced).
3. Income Certificate issued by Revenue Officer / Tehsildar / Form 16.
4. Mark Sheets of Class 10 and Class 12 Board Exams.
5. Aadhaar Card of Applicant.
6. Student Bank Account Passbook (showing Account No & IFSC Code).
7. Passport Size Photograph.

5. APPLICATION PROCESS:
- Online Registration: Register and fill application on the official Bharti Airtel Foundation Scholarship Portal (bhartifoundation.org).
- Document Verification: Upload scanned original certificates.
- Shortlisting & Interview: Shortlisted candidates undergo online interview by Selection Panel.
- Direct Award Sanction: Scholarship amount disbursed directly to institution and student account.
