================================================================================
GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - MINISTRY OF HEALTH & FAMILY WELFARE / NHA
SCHEME GUIDELINES: AYUSHMAN BHARAT - PRADHAN MANTRI JAN AROGYA YOJANA (PM-JAY)
================================================================================
Scheme Code: GOI-HLTH-PMJAY-2024
Category: Healthcare & Health Insurance
Target Beneficiaries: Poor, vulnerable households identified under Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC 2011) and senior citizens aged 70+.

1. SCHEME OBJECTIVE:
To provide cashless secondary and tertiary healthcare hospitalization coverage to the bottom 40% of the Indian population and ensure financial protection against catastrophic health expenditures.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Families identified based on deprivation and occupational criteria in SECC 2011 database:
  * Rural Categories: Households with no male adult member (16-59 yrs), female-headed households, SC/ST households, landless casual manual labor households.
  * Urban Categories: Occupational categories such as ragpickers, beggars, domestic workers, street vendors, cobblers, construction workers, plumbers, transport workers, sanitation workers.
- Senior Citizens: All senior citizens aged 70 years and above (Ayushman Vaya Vandana Card) regardless of income.
- No family size or age cap.

3. BENEFITS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE:
- Health Coverage: Cashless insurance cover of up to ₹5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakh) per family per year.
- Covers pre-hospitalization (up to 3 days) and post-hospitalization (up to 15 days) expenses including diagnostics and medicines.
- Over 1,900 medical procedures covered across empanelled public and private hospitals nationwide.

4. MANDATORY REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Aadhaar Card (For identity and eKYC verification).
2. Ration Card / Family ID / State Food Security Card.
3. Ayushman Card / Golden Card (Generated after eKYC).
4. PM-JAY Eligibility Letter / Family HHID reference number.

5. APPLICATION PROCESS:
- Check Eligibility: Search HHID or Mobile Number on beneficiary.nha.gov.in portal.
- eKYC Verification: Visit nearest Empanelled Hospital (Ayushman Mitradesk) or Common Service Center (CSC).
- Instant Card Generation: Ayushman Card issued immediately after eKYC verification.
