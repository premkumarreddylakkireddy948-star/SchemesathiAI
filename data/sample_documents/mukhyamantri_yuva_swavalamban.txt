================================================================================
GOVERNMENT OF GUJARAT - EDUCATION DEPARTMENT
SCHEME GUIDELINES: MUKHYAMANTRI YUVA SWAVALAMBAN YOJANA (MYSY)
================================================================================
Scheme Code: GUJ-EDU-MYSY-2024
Category: Higher Education & State Scholarship
Target Beneficiaries: Meritorious students pursuing Diploma/Degree courses in Gujarat.

1. SCHEME OBJECTIVE:
To assist bright students from low and middle-income families in Gujarat to pursue higher education degree and diploma courses in Engineering, Medical, Pharmacy, Agriculture, and General streams.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Permanent resident of Gujarat.
- Academic Performance:
  * For Degree Courses: Secured 80 or more percentile in Class 12 (Science/General stream).
  * For Diploma to Degree (D2D) / Diploma Courses: Secured 80 or more percentile in Class 10.
- Annual Family Income Limit: Income from all sources must NOT exceed ₹6,00,000 (Rupees Six Lakh per annum).

3. BENEFITS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE:
- Tuition Fee Subsidy:
  * Medical / Dental (MBBS/BDS): 50% of tuition fee up to ₹2,00,000 per year.
  * Engineering / Pharmacy / Architecture: 50% of tuition fee up to ₹50,000 per year.
  * BSc / BCom / BA / BBA / BCA: 50% of tuition fee up to ₹10,00,00 per year.
  * Diploma Courses: 50% of tuition fee up to ₹25,000 per year.
- Hostel & Boarding Allowance: ₹1,200 per month for 10 months (up to ₹12,000 per year) if studying outside home taluka.
- Book & Equipment Allowance: One-time assistance of ₹5,000 for Medical/Engg and ₹3,000 for Diploma.

4. MANDATORY REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. 10th and 12th Standard Mark Sheets.
2. Admission Letter & Fee Receipt issued by ACPC / University.
3. Self-declaration / Income Certificate from Mamlatdar or TDO.
4. Certificate of Residence in Gujarat (Domicile Certificate).
5. Bank Account Passbook (Aadhaar linked).
6. Aadhaar Card.
7. Hostel Fee Receipt & Warden Certificate (for hostel allowance).

5. APPLICATION PROCESS:
- Online Registration: Register on mysy.guj.nic.in portal.
- Help Center Verification: Verify original documents at designated Help Centers (Government Colleges).
- Renewal: Annual renewal based on maintaining minimum 50% marks in semester exams and 75% attendance.
