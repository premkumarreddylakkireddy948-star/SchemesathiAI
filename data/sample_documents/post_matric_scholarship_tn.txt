================================================================================
GOVERNMENT OF TAMIL NADU - HIGHER EDUCATION DEPARTMENT
SCHEME GUIDELINES: POST-MATRIC SCHOLARSHIP FOR SC/ST/SCC STUDENTS
================================================================================
Scheme Code: TN-HE-PMS-2024
Category: Higher Education & Scholarships
Target Beneficiaries: Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), and SC converted to Buddhism (SCC) students in Tamil Nadu.

1. SCHEME OBJECTIVE:
To provide financial assistance to Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), and SC Converted to Buddhism (SCC) students studying at post-matriculation or post-secondary stage to enable them to complete their education in recognized college degree courses, diplomas, and professional studies.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Applicant must be a permanent resident and domicile of Tamil Nadu.
- Must belong to SC, ST, or SC Converted to Buddhism (SCC) community.
- Annual Family Income Limit:
  * SC / ST Students: Total annual family income from all sources must NOT exceed ₹2,50,000 (Rupees Two Lakh Fifty Thousand per annum).
  * SCC Students: Total annual family income must NOT exceed ₹2,50,000.
- Course Level: Must be pursuing Post-Matriculation courses (Class 11, Class 12, ITI, Diploma, Undergraduate BA/BSc/BCom, Engineering B.E/B.Tech, Medical MBBS, Post-Graduate MA/MSc/MCom, PhD) in government, government-aided, or self-financing recognized institutions.
- Attendance: Minimum 75% attendance in the current academic year.

3. BENEFITS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE:
- 100% Tuition Fee Reimbursement for government-quota and self-financing seats as per government fee committee norms.
- Compulsory non-refundable fee coverage (special fee, exam fee, library fee).
- Monthly Maintenance Allowance:
  * Hostellers: ₹740 to ₹1,200 per month depending on course group.
  * Day Scholars: ₹380 to ₹550 per month.
- Book and Thesis allowance for research scholars.

4. MANDATORY REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Community Certificate (Permanent Smart Card / E-Certificate issued by Tahsildar).
2. Income Certificate (Issued by Revenue Department / Tahsildar within the last 12 months).
3. Educational Mark Sheets (10th, 12th, or previous semester mark sheets).
4. Aadhaar Card (Must be active and seeded with applicant's bank account).
5. Student Bank Account Passbook front page (showing Account No, IFSC Code, Branch Name).
6. College Admission Fee Receipt & Bonafide Certificate signed by College Principal.
7. Recent Passport Size Photograph.

5. APPLICATION PROCESS:
- Online Application: Register on the Tamil Nadu State Scholarship Portal (TNPASS / electronic scholarship portal).
- Institutional Verification: Submit hard copy of documents to College Nodal Officer for verification.
- Approval: District Welfare Officer (Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare Dept) sanctions scholarship directly to student DBTL account.
