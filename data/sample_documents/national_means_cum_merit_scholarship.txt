================================================================================
GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - MINISTRY OF EDUCATION
SCHEME GUIDELINES: NATIONAL MEANS-CUM-MERIT SCHOLARSHIP SCHEME (NMMSS)
================================================================================
Scheme Code: GOI-EDU-NMMSS-2024
Category: School Education & Scholarships
Target Beneficiaries: Meritorious students from economically weaker sections studying in Class 8.

1. SCHEME OBJECTIVE:
To award scholarships to meritorious students of economically weaker sections to arrest their drop out at Class 8 and encourage them to continue study at secondary stage.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Student must be studying as a regular student in Class 8 in a Government, Local Body, or Government-aided school.
- Marks Criteria: Must have secured minimum 55% marks or equivalent grade in Class 7 examination (5% relaxation for SC/ST students).
- Parental Income Limit: Total annual income of parents from all sources must NOT exceed ₹3,50,000 (Rupees Three Lakh Fifty Thousand per annum).
- Students of NVS, KVS, Sainik schools and private residential schools are NOT eligible.

3. BENEFITS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE:
- Scholarship Amount: ₹12,000 per annum (₹1,000 per month).
- Duration: Awarded for 4 years from Class 9 to Class 12.
- Payment Mode: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into student's bank account via Public Financial Management System (PFMS).

4. MANDATORY REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Income Certificate of parents issued by designated state authority.
2. Class 7 Mark Sheet / Report Card certified by School Headmaster.
3. Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC) if claiming reservation quota.
4. Disability Certificate issued by competent medical board (if applicable).
5. Aadhaar Card of the student.
6. Bank Passbook copy of student (Joint account with parent or single active account).

5. APPLICATION PROCESS:
- State Selection Test: Appear for the State Level NMMSS Examination (Mental Ability Test MAT & Scholastic Aptitude Test SAT).
- NSP Registration: Selected candidates apply online on National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in).
- Verification: Verification by Institute Nodal Officer (INO) and State Nodal Officer (SNO).
